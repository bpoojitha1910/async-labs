# Task Submission – Poojitha

## What I built

This submission was created for the Async Labs T7 – Growth & Advertising challenge.

I developed two distinct advertising campaign concepts for the 7.5-inch smart workspace display, each based on a different selling angle. The concepts I explored were customization and productivity.

For the selected concept, I created advertising assets designed an Instagram carousel and a static advertisement.

### Campaign Concepts

**Concept 1 – One Display. Endless Possibilities.**  
A customization-focused campaign that positions the display as a flexible product that can adapt to different users and workflows.

**Concept 2 – Too Much to Track. One Place to Look.**  
A productivity-focused campaign that addresses the problem of important information being scattered across calendars, tasks, messages, and applications.
